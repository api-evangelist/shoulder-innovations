# Shoulder Innovations

> Commercial-stage medical technology company in Grand Rapids, Michigan, focused exclusively on
> the shoulder surgical care market. Publicly listed since July 2025 under the ticker SI.

Shoulder Innovations develops the InSet(R) shoulder arthroplasty portfolio, built around inset
glenoid fixation designed to reduce the "rocking horse" forces implicated in glenoid implant
failure. It also publishes ProVoyance(R) Shoulder, a first-party AI/ML pre-operative planning
application that reconstructs 3D bone models from CT imaging.

## API surface

## Products

- [InSet Total Shoulder Arthroplasty](https://shoulderinnovations.com/inset-total-shoulder-system/): anatomic total shoulder system built on the InSet glenoid.
- [InSet Reverse Shoulder Arthroplasty](https://shoulderinnovations.com/inset-reverse-shoulder-system/): reverse system intended to preserve rotator cuff function.
- [InSet Glenoid](https://shoulderinnovations.com/inset-glenoid/): the inset fixation glenoid component the portfolio is built around.
- [I-Series Humeral Stems](https://shoulderinnovations.com/humeral-stem/): including the InSet 70 and InSet 95 humeral stems and the stemless system.
- [InSet Tray System](https://shoulderinnovations.com/inset-tray-system/): one-tray TSA and two-tray RSA instrumentation.
- [ProVoyance Shoulder](https://shoulderinnovations.com/provoyance-shoulder/): AI/ML pre-operative planning software; access via a customer portal.
